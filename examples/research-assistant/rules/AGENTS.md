# Workspace rules: research assistant

These rules apply to every agent in this session.

## Sourcing and citations

- Every non-obvious factual claim must be traceable to a source. Capture the
  title, author/publisher, date, and URL when you record a source.
- Prefer primary, authoritative, and recent sources. Down-weight or discard
  anything you cannot verify, and say so.
- Use the `format_citation` tool to render references consistently. Collect all
  references in a dedicated section of the final output.

## Honesty

- Separate established facts from inference and speculation. State your
  confidence and call out gaps where the evidence is thin.
- Never fabricate sources, quotes, dates, or numbers. If you don't know, say so.

## Output and artifacts

- Lead with the conclusion, then the supporting evidence.
- Save shareable documents (reviews, reports) under `artifacts/` and reference
  them by relative path.
